## Pre-onboarding priority
Lessons 01–12 and 14 are core for general junior contribution. Treat Lessons 13, 15–19 as expandable and Lesson 20 as a final simulation. Do not rush past tests to finish all 20.

## Passing criteria
Correct tests, an unassisted explanation, an edge case, and a small modification. Scores are reviewed by you and the tutor; no automatic self-awarded mastery.
